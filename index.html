About Me

Despite a decent academic score and the expectation to follow the conventional route, I chose to follow my passion for marketing and entered a polytechnic — not the popular choice, and one that came with judgment and uncertainty. Today, I'm pursuing a Bachelor's in Business Administration at the National University of Singapore, majoring in Marketing — diving into B2B & B2C marketing, brand strategy, product, go-to-market strategy, content creation, customer experience and entrepreneurship.

I actively seek opportunities, embrace unconventional paths, and step outside my comfort zone. That's taken me across New York, China and Vietnam, picking up insights into different working cultures, business environments and startup ecosystems along the way — and it's sharpened my interest in startups, partnerships, and building meaningful collaborations across borders. Lately, I've been spending more time learning about AI and how it applies to business and partnerships.
